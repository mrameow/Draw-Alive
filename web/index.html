    ❓ How to play

    1. 🎨 Draw any creature (facing left) and press Bring to life.
    2. 🖍️ Colour-in: pick an animal outline, colour it with the brush or the 🪣 fill – it's cut out along the outline automatically.
    3. 📷 Photo: choose a picture, use the camera, or just paste one (Ctrl+V). The background is removed for you – touch up with Keep / Remove.
    4. ＋ at the top adds another environment (Ocean, Forest, Space, Meadow, Snow or your own photo). Every environment keeps its own creatures.
    5. Drag creatures to move them. Tap one to change size, speed, how it moves (walk, hop, bounce, dash, slither, swim, fly…), make it do a trick, flip, copy or delete it.
    6. Creatures do little tricks by themselves and sometimes play together – they wave, chase, dance and high-five.
    7. ▦ at the top shows all your worlds at once. Tap one to jump into it.
    8. 📡 Share: make one device the main screen, then other phones, tablets or computers join with its code (or QR code) and send their worlds to it – live.
    9. 🖼️ Save picture keeps a snapshot of your world. Everything is saved automatically on this device.

    Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Del delete creature · Space pause · Esc close
